Genie Certification Test Log

The tests behind my PRD, "Certified should mean checked, on this version." Shatakshi Chaudhari, Oct 30–31, 2026, on the bakery sample data. Agent IDs and emails are removed.

Main finding: the benchmark caught a real change in behavior, and the Certified badge didn't react. T1 and T2 show this. The rest show why a benchmark score alone can't fix it.

T1

Badge after a manual edit

Setup
Clone of my agent. Assigned Certified, then saved the instruction Always round revenue to the nearest $1,000.
  • After reload the agent was still Certified. The tooltip said only "This asset is certified," with no date, version or certifier.
  • The Agents list and Discover showed the Certified tag beside "Modified 22 seconds ago" and "Modified 44 seconds ago."
T2

Pressure test on a certified agent, run by hand

Setup
The same clone, Certified, with the rounding line added on purpose. 8 benchmark questions. Run by hand, Oct 31, about 12:45–1:15 AM.
  • Agent mode scored 4/8 and Chat mode scored 4/8, with different questions passing in each.
  • Improve with Genie correctly traced 4 revenue failures to the rounding line, and asked whether to keep it.
  • I skipped its question. It said it would "focus on the improvements I can make without that answer," then replaced 2 benchmarks: #3 best-selling product and #5 top franchise. The changes saved on the certified agent.
  • It re-ran and reported "5 out of 8 passing, up from 4," which compares two different question sets.
  • It acknowledged run-to-run variation: #7 total revenue went fail → pass, and #4 payment method went pass → fail.
  • #4 returned all 3 methods plus revenue, with Mastercard on top. #6 returned the top 5 days. Both contained the right answer and were graded as failures.
  • Afterward the Agents list still showed Certified, and the Modified time had updated.

T4

Starter Space scores 0/4 on correct answers

Setup
The sample agent's shipped benchmark: 4 rewordings of one question, Agent mode, Oct 30.
  • Score 0/4. Every text answer named the right product (Golden Gate Ginger, $11,595; 3,865 units).
  • Reasons given: Extra Rows, Formatting, Incomplete Output, because Genie returned a ranked list rather than one row.
  • The failure analysis itself said the top product "appears correct."
  • Control: 2 benchmarks worded with a fixed shape ("Show the top 5…") scored 2/2 on my agent.

T8

What certification and version history offer today

  • Assign certification is a choice of Certified, Deprecated or None. It shows and requires no benchmark score, run or evidence.
  • The clock icon in the agent header opens past chats, not versions.
  • The ⋮ menu has Export to metric view, Clone, Assign certification, Move to trash and Send feedback. There is no history, restore or undo.
  • Instructions are one text box. A line added by the AI helper sits among the others with no author, date or earlier version.
T9

Cost of testing

  • system.billing.usage showed about 98.9 Genie DBU over Oct 29–31, of which 55.03 came from the agent with the 8-question benchmark. It also includes that agent's chats.
  • At the $0.07/DBU list price, the total is about $6.90 (calculation). Usage is tagged by surface and agent, but benchmark runs aren't separated from chats.
T10

Where Genie behaved well

  • Asked about low stock, Genie said no inventory table exists and offered sales velocity as a proxy.
  • Asked for revenue last month, it found no data for those dates, checked the real range (May 1–17, 2024) and said so.
  • In T2, Improve with Genie correctly diagnosed the rounding line as the cause of the failures.

Limits of this evidence

  • One person, one workspace, sample data, and a small number of runs. These tests show what the product does. They don't show how often it happens to customers.
  • I ran T2 and its audit check by hand. The other tests were run with an AI browser assistant.
  • The edits in T1 and T2 were deliberate. How often certified agents get edited in real use is an assumption to measure in a beta.
  • Not tested: whether a manual Good/Bad override carries over to the next run, and the "Attempt fix" link in Chat mode.
  • The certification action wasn't visible in the audit log when queried, because the log was lagging.
